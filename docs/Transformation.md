## Transformation
This file includes the transformations made and also the use of those transformations in various analysis and future ml processes.
## Date Transformation
The **Order Date** column was used to derive general purpose attributes, We have extracted the following attributes from order date column.
- Year : Which can be helpful for segeregating the sales data specific to year
- Month and Month_name : The both numerical and month name can be used to segeregate the sales across the months of a year.
- Day of week : The day of week can help us segeregate and analyse the sales transaction over the week, respect to weekdays and weekends.
